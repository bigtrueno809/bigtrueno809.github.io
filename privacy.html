What this app does

Dino Egg Catch is a game. Help Mama Dino catch falling eggs, dodge meteors, and unlock looks in the Mama Dino Locker across 4 levels.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal information.

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • Version 1 has no ads and no in-app purchases.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party data collection.
  • The app does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, or photos.

Scores, total eggs saved, best score, and shop progress are saved only on your device.

Children's privacy

Dino Egg Catch is a family game and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because no personal information is collected, there is nothing for a parent to request to review or delete from the app itself.

If you contact us

If you email support, we will use your message only to reply to you. That email is not sold, shared for advertising, or added to a mailing list.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds ads, in-app purchases, accounts, or analytics, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the updated policy will be posted at the same URL.
