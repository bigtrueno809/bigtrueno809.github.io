About the game

Help Mama Dino save her babies! Catch falling eggs in your basket, dodge flaming meteors, and work your way through 4 levels — Gentle, Faster, Volcano Rush, and Lava Storm. Unlock 5 looks in the Mama Dino Locker as you save more eggs, from Classic Green all the way to Golden Mama. Your total eggs and best score are saved on your device.

Version 1 is clean: no ads, no in-app purchases.

How to play

  • Move Mama Dino by dragging, or with Arrow Keys / WASD on a keyboard.
  • Catch falling eggs to save them.
  • Dodge meteors — they cost you a life.
  • Unlock new Mama Dino looks in the Locker as you progress.

Troubleshooting

  • Game looks wrong or too dark: Version 1 forces light appearance. Make sure your iPhone is on a current iOS version, close the app fully, and reopen it.
  • Game won't start: Close the app fully and reopen it. If it still won't start, restart your iPhone and try again.
  • Progress or a look seems missing: Tell us which level and which look in your message, and what you were doing just before it happened.
